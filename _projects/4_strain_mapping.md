Nanobeam electron diffraction strain mapping usually measures the positions of individual Bragg disks, throwing away most of the information
in each diffraction pattern. I developed a whole-pattern-fitting method that usees all of the available information for improved strain mapping.

These methods are being adopted for semiconductor metrology workflows at Samsung, and I supervise their ongoing development.
