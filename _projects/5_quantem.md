[quantEM](https://github.com/electronmicroscopy/quantem) is an open-source Python library for quantitative electron microscopy,
developed by the Ophus group and collaborators. I created the package and am a core developer: I lead the **ptychography** and **machine
learning** modules and contributed core infrastructure (data structures, visualization, utilities, and configuration).

- Source: [github.com/electronmicroscopy/quantem](https://github.com/electronmicroscopy/quantem)
- Tutorials: [github.com/electronmicroscopy/quantem-tutorials](https://github.com/electronmicroscopy/quantem-tutorials)

The [interactive ptychography demo]({{ '/interactive/ptychography/' | relative_url }}) runs the same pixelated multislice approach used in
quantEM, live in your browser.
