Professional Summary

Research scientist working at the intersection of machine learning and electron microscopy. I build self-supervised and physics-informed methods for inverse problems in scientific imaging (deep generative priors, implicit neural representations, and differentiable forward models) and ship them as open-source tools used across the field. I am currently developing multimodal vision transformer foundation models for electron microscopy, pretrained on large simulated diffraction datasets together with crystal structure and composition, as part of a $1.2M Deep Material Priors program with Toyota Research Institute. Earlier work produced >10× faster and 40% higher-resolution ptychographic reconstructions and is being transferred into semiconductor metrology workflows.
